**How it was found:** every second of every video was read with text recognition (OCR, with an extra pass for dark-mode screens). Emails, phone numbers, contact-list headers and known contact names were located and blurred, with a margin of about 1.3 s before and after and extra space around them for scrolling.

**How it was checked:** all 36 blurred videos were OCR-scanned again from scratch. The 3 misses this found (Online Classroom Management 5:56–6:33, Productivity 3:51–3:55, Where to Search 2:40–2:42) were fixed and re-checked. The final scan finds **no readable email address or phone number** in any video, apart from one placeholder (`yourname@gmail`). Blurred frames were also spot-checked by eye.

Scripts: `scripts/blur/` (detection, window building, rendering, verification). Blur boxes per video are in `data/blur_windows/` (times and coordinates only, no personal text).

## How it was done (reproducible)

- `scripts/probe.py`: technical check (loudness, per-channel level, silence, black, freeze). Raw results are in `data/probe_results.jsonl`.
- `scripts/norm.sh`: two-pass EBU R128 loudness normalisation to -16 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP, 80 Hz high-pass, mono output, video stream copied.
- `scripts/batch_audio_fix.sh`: the 52 audio-only fixes.
- `scripts/meet2_sync.py`: voice-to-video sync map for Meet part 2 (English translation of the voice is in `data/meet2_voice_transcript_en.json`).
- `scripts/special_edits.sh`: the Module 1 join, the Module 17 slide-6 splice, the LinkedIn blur and the dead-air trims.
